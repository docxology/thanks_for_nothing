# Model authority and inference contract

[Packaged GNN declarations](../src/thanks_for_nothing/models) own numerical
choices and experiment settings. [Generated current facts](../output/reports/research_summary.md)
and the manuscript's populated settings table expose the executed values.
Guide prose, captions and plotting code are not another parameter authority.

## Complete standalone generative laws

The [standalone GNN compiler](../src/thanks_for_nothing/standalone_gnn.py)
materializes complete `.gnn.md` models before their tensors reach a numerical
consumer. The pipeline retains these files in `output/specifications/instances`
and reads the preserved bytes through the pinned GNN parser, strict semantic
admission and canonical POMDP builder. These are executable categorical models.

Coverage includes stratum-specific recall worlds, well/poor news channels,
the fixed attention process, practice observation schedules and restricted prior,
the learned-state transfer embedding, open/restricted scene models, and both
diagnostic and label-only joint query models with open/restricted support.
Scene and joint models declare the full query-conditioned likelihood and controlled
transition tensor. The joint state includes the known query and latent basis;
actions set the query while preserving the latent candidate. Finite task
consumers obtain conditional channels and priors from these admitted arrays.

`data/generative_models.json` binds each complete model to its actual consumer,
use count, originating GNN versions and numerical evidence. Identical complete
laws share an instance while retaining every originating version, including
sensitivity declarations, in `specifications/origins`. Native GNN renders and
parsed graphs cover every instance. Complete cards provide every tensor entry;
the page-sized inventory distinguishes authoring inputs from full instances.

Learned per-agent parameters remain source-bound fitted data under the declared
learning and transfer families. Independent mathematical oracles remain independent
controls. Archived notebook reconstructions retain their original behavior and
source custody; the compiler does not rewrite historical source code.

The [analysis declaration](../src/thanks_for_nothing/models/analysis.gnn.md)
extends this boundary to inferential levels, statistical stream offsets,
sample-size diagnostic prefixes, notebook budgets and source-replay adapter
defaults. Altering a selected declaration changes the consumed protocol and
its preserved record. Mathematical fixture distributions, original-source
digest/dimension pins, structural policy definitions and shared visual constants
remain independent checks or definitions. They are not fitted to model outputs.

## Admission before computation

The [graph adapter](../src/thanks_for_nothing/gnn_graphs.py) reads variables,
dimensions and edges from the pinned GNN Markdown parser after numerical model
admission. It calls GNN's native network renderer in a fresh staging directory
for every declaration and requires both an image and topology statistics.
The generated `data/gnn_graphs.json` binds these products to the preserved
declarations, executing parser/renderer modules and consumed numerical records.
Missing output cannot be replaced by a stale image from an earlier render.
Admission also reruns the native renderer in isolated staging and compares
decoded pixels and complete statistics with the retained products. Parsed edge
directions independently determine the directed/undirected counts; repairing
a catalog hash does not admit a blank image, another model's image or forged
orientation statistics.

The manuscript's [graphical-model section](manuscript/supplement.md#sec-gnn-graphs)
shows all distinct parsed topologies and an automatically populated tensor and
consumer inventory. Native attention executes through GNN/pymdp; finite learners,
comparators and inquiry tasks consume admitted arrays through their named local
numerical interfaces. The analysis model carries the statistical protocol.
These roles are distinct even though the declarations share a backend label.
The opening GNN model-card page presents a time-unrolled Bayesian tensor
definition beside this parsed graph and an injected consumer inventory. Its
complete HTML/JSON companion retains every admitted tensor and scientific
parameter, the original declaration text, native assets, execution bindings and
dependency revision receipts. It does not identify finite task-specific laws
with the common tensor representation.
Graph cycles express declaration dependencies rather than an instantaneous
causal DAG. Native attention receipt dimensions and action bounds derive from
the actual tensors; a real changed-cardinality test exercises both executor paths.

The package calls pinned Realizing Emptiness `modeling.registry.parse_model`,
which consumes the real pinned GNN Markdown parser, semantic validator and
canonical POMDP builder. Pydantic boundaries reject missing/extra parameters,
invalid probabilities, wrong shapes and non-normalized channels. Preserved
declaration hashes bind the execution. Alternate specification directories
support real file-based interventions and rejecting tests.

GNN A is observation × state; B is next × previous × action; C is log preference;
D is the initial categorical prior. The corrected passive learner uses admitted
A/B/D; its passive C has no action-selection role. Native attention consumes
A/B/C/D through the actual GNN/pymdp executor. New finite evaluators consume
admitted parameters and independent evidence oracles; a dependency import alone
is not evidence that a backend executed an experiment.

## Corrected recall and independent assessment

Each independently seeded agent-world unit receives shared environmental states
and observations across paired conditions. The true world transition depends
on its fixed stratum, while the agent's transition is separately declared.
Assigning a recall intervention does not change that process.

Proper Dirichlet counts initialize the agent. Each observed event yields a
normalized state posterior; the admitted learning increment is added only to
the observed row. Column normalization gives the learned observation channel.
Daily state priors reset as declared; learned counts persist. The state prior
is not silently learned or overwritten with certainty.

Positive replay selects eligible observed events without replacement and
re-evaluates them using stored original priors and the current learned channel.
The powered likelihood is not renormalized over observation columns; this is
the specified generalized-Bayes update. Random recall matches replay dose while
changing selection. No recall and absence valuation share inference exactly.
Counterfactual value is calculated separately and never manufactures an event.

Independent streams supply environmental exposure, replay, held-out evaluation
and optimism assessment. Frozen models are evaluated without training on test
observations. Binary Brier uses the well-state squared error; observation log
loss is predictive observation loss, not latent-state posterior loss. Entropy
does not measure correctness.

The optimism task uses its own admitted normalized news matrices and prior
bounds. Its mode mixture depends on the stipulated readout. These assumptions
are part of the model; a change in that score is not an independently validated
human optimism or well-being measure.

## Distinct operational meanings of nothing

The absence fixture compares an admitted detector's no-event token with a
missing-observation sentinel that preserves the prior by rule. A uniform
likelihood is a no-information control. Counterfactual value has zero-reference
and zero-cost nulls. Consistent hidden-label permutation preserves predictive
probabilities. That invariance is a mathematical comparator for non-reification,
not a Buddhist realization criterion.

Realizing Emptiness contributes actual KL, Blackwell, QRF/support and finite
evidence interfaces. [The practice guide](emptiness-practice.md) distinguishes
metadata and in-sample upstream diagnostics from the new enacted policy and
independent assessment. Both posteriors are fitted separately when structural
support changes.

## Scene sampling and joint structural inquiry

[The gaze guide](gaze-experiments.md) separates original attention reconstruction,
learned-channel transfer and active scene inquiry. Registration and regional
channels are authored; painting pixels never enter inference. Source controlled
transition axes are retained in their original order and explicitly adapted.

[The scene/encoding guide](boundary-gaze-design.md) specifies diagnostic and
label-only observation laws, spatial versus action-response queries, a shared
decision budget, actual restricted support and the explicit reference intervention.
The independent uniform prior and admitted cyclic maps are necessary for its
visual-only non-identification result. No claim generalizes that result to
arbitrary scene priors or biological vision.
