## A window of inquiry

A declared process generates calibration observations. A policy chooses activity
using opportunity value, cost and its previous effective gain. Activity can
make the alternative family available, or access can be blocked. If accessible,
the agent fits restricted and open posteriors separately and compares evidence.
It selects the open working family only when the stable log-evidence comparison
favors it; exact ties retain the restriction.

Only afterward are fresh assessment outcomes revealed. Log loss is reported in
nats per outcome, with expenditure separate. Rigid, inquiry, no-preference, sham
and no-access policies share complete exposure and potential-outcome identities.
The sham can pay for activity without changing its forecast.

The logistic action rule is a declared project policy, not a complete canonical
expected-free-energy planner. Its initial value and cost are confounded through
their difference. Source agreement or successful replay does not identify a
human gratitude parameter.

## Retention, revision and costs

A stationary process can favor a useful working restriction. A reversal can
make an excluded candidate relevant. The agent can retain or reuse a useful
family while future alternatives remain examinable. This distinguishes using a
representation from making its restriction compulsory.

Useful-prior retention, candidate permutations, exact ties, blocked access and
unchanged sham forecasts are genuine controls. Inquiry costs can remain without
a predictive gain. No gate requires an advantage for openness or activity.
[Exhaustive count analysis](exact-decisions.md) retains rare adverse selections
that seeded trajectories may miss.
