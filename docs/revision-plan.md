# Research revision plan

This file preserves the exploratory development sequence of completed earlier
revisions. It is not preregistration or the authority for current defaults.
Current quantities are [generated](../output/reports/research_summary.md), and
[current guides](README.md) describe the active contracts.

The initial revision below starts from private release 0.1.0. Its results have already been
inspected; the additional analyses are exploratory, not preregistered discoveries.
Preserve the source notebooks, preprint, dependency pins, main study exposures,
eight primary endpoint contrasts and their Holm family.

1. Add studentized bootstrap intervals using within-stratum variance estimates
   for every bootstrap resample. Report unavailable intervals when the observed
   sample or any resample has zero variance; never discard problematic draws.
   Retain percentile and analytic t intervals on the same resamples.
2. Add world-specific paired contrasts for Brier loss, observation log loss and
   the optimism readout. These are descriptive, pointwise exploratory intervals;
   do not infer subgroup significance from unadjusted coverage.
3. Report how equal-grid estimates depend on the distribution of world strata:
   leave-one-stratum-out means and the convex weighting envelope. These are
   sensitivity ranges conditional on stratum estimates, not confidence intervals.
4. Broaden operating-characteristic controls to symmetric heavy tails, skewed
   differences, heterogeneous strata and discrete boundary cases. Retain failures
   of symmetry assumptions, coverage, interval availability and Monte Carlo
   precision. Increase the declared repeated experiments and draws without
   selecting a seed or method based on its performance.
5. Redesign every figure at the actual manuscript width, separating distributions,
   estimation uncertainty, deterministic controls and weighting sensitivity.
   Use semantic colors plus markers or labels, explicit units, zero references,
   denominators and source-bound vector/raster export.
6. Deepen primary-source scholarship and connect each literature to an operation,
   a limitation and a falsifiable empirical contrast. Distinguish predictive loss
   from calibration in isolation and preserve all access ceilings.

Acceptance requires real mathematical and rejecting controls, both supported
Python endpoint suites with at least 90% coverage, strict lint/types, rebuilt
wheel and SDIST qualification, inspection of every final manuscript page/figure,
desktop/mobile HTML QA and an independent reviewer of the immutable final cut.
Delivery remains private and versioned; hosted CI is a separate execution state.
