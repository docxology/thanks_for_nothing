## Five source dossiers

### 1. Costly gratitude: Bartlett and DeSteno (2006)

**Identity:** Monica Y. Bartlett and David DeSteno, “Gratitude and Prosocial Behavior: Helping When It Costs You,” *Psychological Science* 17(4), 319–325. DOI [10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01705.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01705.x). Crossref splits the subtitle; the full publisher PDF confirms it.

**Access and locators:** Published full-text PDF from [UC Berkeley](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/images/uploads/Gratitude_and_Prosocial_Behavior_Helping_When_It_Costs_You.pdf), targeted inspection of Study 1 methods/results, pp. 320–322; Studies 2–3, pp. 322–324; General Discussion, pp. 324–325. Receipts: `bartlett2006.pdf`, `bartlett2006.txt`, `bartlett2006-crossref.json`.

**Supported claim:** Interpersonally induced gratitude increased costly helping compared with neutral/amusement conditions; Study 3's source-attribution manipulation curtailed incidental helping of strangers. This supports distinguishing gratitude's motivational/social consequences from pleasant affect alone.

**Boundary:** These laboratory helping studies do not test contemplative inquiry, benefactor-free appreciation, or emptiness. Their transfer to inquiry is our hypothesis, not their result. Mediation analyses alone do not exclude every rival explanation.

**Placement:** Add one sentence after the first paragraph of “Gratitude, appreciation and relational meaning”; use the source-attribution boundary when motivating matched affect and social-expectancy controls in “Limitations and falsifiable future studies.”

### 2. Distinct practice families: Dahl, Lutz and Davidson (2015)

**Identity:** Cortland J. Dahl, Antoine Lutz and Richard J. Davidson, “Reconstructing and deconstructing the self: cognitive mechanisms in meditation practice,” *Trends in Cognitive Sciences* 19(9), 515–523. DOI [10.1016/j.tics.2015.07.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2015.07.001).

**Access and locators:** [PMC author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4595910/), full text acquired; targeted inspection of “Constructive Family: Perspective Taking and Reappraisal,” “Deconstructive Family: Self-inquiry and Insight,” Boxes 1–2, and “Concluding Remarks and Future Directions.” Receipts: `dahl2015-pmc.html`, `dahl2015-plain.txt`, `dahl2015-crossref.json`.

**Supported claim:** Their proposed taxonomy distinguishes attention/meta-awareness, constructive reappraisal/perspective-taking, and deconstructive self-inquiry. Meta-awareness can enable inquiry while remaining a different process. Their conclusion explicitly retains ethical, interpersonal and cultural context.

**Boundary:** This is a framework proposal with acknowledged empirical gaps. It does not establish that gratitude causes deconstructive insight. Mapping the present appreciation operation onto constructive preparation is our synthesis.

**Placement:** Insert a brief taxonomy paragraph between “Gratitude for the conditions of realization” and “Gratitude, appreciation and relational meaning”; revise the literature-to-model table so access and inquiry are not merged into a generic practice variable.

### 3. Access versus control: Sandved-Smith and colleagues (2021)

**Identity:** Lars Sandved-Smith, Casper Hesp, Jérémie Mattout, Karl Friston, Antoine Lutz and Maxwell J. D. Ramstead, “Towards a computational phenomenology of mental action: modelling meta-awareness and attentional control with deep parametric active inference,” *Neuroscience of Consciousness* 2021(1), niab018. DOI [10.1093/nc/niab018](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niab018).

**Access and locators:** [PMC published full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8396119/) via Europe PMC XML; targeted sections `s1-s2` (opacity/transparency), `s2-s3`, `s3-s3`, `s4-s1` (access/control distinction), and `s4-s2`. Receipts: `sandvedsmith2021.xml`, `sandvedsmith2021-readable.txt`, `sandvedsmith2021-crossref.json`.

**Supported claim:** Their simulated architecture separates perceptual, attentional and meta-awareness levels; access to internal states and control of those states are distinct. Empirical fitting remains future work.

**Boundary:** The present binary access manipulation is a simpler authored mechanism, not an implementation or validation of this three-level model. Decentering/opacification terminology does not establish realization.

**Placement:** Cite beside the access/control distinction in “Enacted inquiry toward model revisability” and the proposed process measures in “Limitations and falsifiable future studies.”

**Metadata correction:** UCL's record says issue 2. The [publisher's correction](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niab035), preserved as `sandvedsmith2021-note.xml`, explicitly reassigns the paper to issue 1; Crossref agrees.

### 4. A substantive theoretical contrast: Laukkonen and Slagter (2021)

**Identity:** Ruben E. Laukkonen and Heleen A. Slagter, “From many to (n)one: Meditation and the plasticity of the predictive mind,” *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 128, 199–217. DOI [10.1016/j.neubiorev.2021.06.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2021.06.021).

**Access and locators:** Published PDF from [Southern Cross University's institutional record](https://researchportal.scu.edu.au/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/From-many-to-none-Meditation-and/991013062213702368). Targeted inspection: §3.3, pp. 205–206; discussion/future directions, pp. 212–214; §6.2. Receipts: `laukkonen2021-scu.pdf`, `laukkonen2021.txt`, `laukkonen2021-crossref.json`. The institutional PDF adds a cover page; use printed page numbers.

**Supported claim:** Their predictive-processing theory proposes progressively reduced temporal/conceptual depth, including a radical nondual endpoint. It explicitly acknowledges differences among Buddhist traditions and meanings of nonduality.

**Boundary/counterargument:** Better predictive switching is neither that endpoint nor an agreed definition of emptiness. Their account is a theory/review, not empirical proof of such an endpoint; its practice continuum is not a hierarchy of superior traditions. This comparison sharpens, rather than closes, the present philosophical gap.

**Placement:** Add a contrasting paragraph in “Non-reification, precision and the limits of a finite counterpart,” retaining useful everyday representation as this manuscript's declared target rather than attributing equivalence to the source.

### 5. Identification before interpretation: Wilson and Collins (2019)

**Identity:** Robert C. Wilson and Anne G. E. Collins, “Ten simple rules for the computational modeling of behavioral data,” *eLife* 8, e49547. DOI [10.7554/eLife.49547](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.49547).

**Access and locators:** [PMC published full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6879303/) via Europe PMC XML. Targeted sections `s7` (parameter recovery), `s8` (model recovery), `s8-4` (candidate-set limitation), `s10` (validation), and Appendix 3. Receipts: `wilson2019.xml`, `wilson2019-readable.txt`, `wilson2019-crossref.json`. The separate Berkeley manuscript is supplemental access, not the version used for locators.

**Supported claim:** Recoverability depends on the parameter regime; model recovery uses a confusion matrix. Winning a relative comparison does not establish absolute adequacy. Generating behavior from fitted models tests effects that a likelihood comparison can miss.

**Boundary:** Exact finite evidence sums do not establish human parameter identification or philosophical validity. Human fitting and model-recovery analyses remain future requirements unless independently implemented and verified.

**Placement:** Cite in “Limitations and falsifiable future studies” after the mechanism-testing paragraph; distinguish software replay from parameter recovery, and within-family selection from adequate generative behavior.

## Argument integration map — our synthesis

| Current manuscript heading | Concrete integration | Disconfirming observation or unresolved boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gratitude for the conditions of realization | Define gratitude as a proposed motivational orientation toward enabling conditions; distinguish constructive preparation, access and inquiry. | Appreciation changes reports but not costly inquiry, or the same behavior follows from generic curiosity. |
| Gratitude, appreciation and relational meaning | Use the costly-helping study to motivate measurement of social attribution and action, separately from positive affect. | Matched positive affect or reciprocity expectations fully account for behavior. |
| From literature to model tests | Give motivation, access, revision, prediction and realization separate rows or columns. | No one scalar validates all links; a failure at one link must remain visible. |
| Enacted inquiry toward model revisability | Say explicitly that opening the candidate set is a finite intervention on availability; internal recognition of that availability requires separate evidence. | Choices change because options appear, without evidence of metacognitive access or inquiry. |
| Non-reification, precision and the limits of a finite counterpart | Contrast retaining useful representations with theories about reduced conceptual depth; identify the philosophical commitment being bracketed. | An agent can improve reversal prediction while treating its newest representation as intrinsically established. Thus flexibility is insufficient. |
| Limitations and falsifiable future studies | Require competing motivational models, parameter/model recovery and generated behavioral checks before interpreting fitted parameters. | Models cannot be recovered in the observed parameter range, or the winning model misses the key behavior. |

The strongest revision is to state the direction of explanation precisely: appreciation may motivate examination; access may enable examination; evidence can support retention or revision; independent prediction evaluates a finite consequence. The philosophical target concerns how distinctions are taken to exist, not simply whether they can be switched. That last sentence is this paper's interpretive commitment and must be argued from its emptiness sources, not claimed as a consequence of the five additions.

A useful short insertion in the Discussion would be: “The finite result concerns the availability and use of alternative descriptions. A system could perform those operations while reifying whichever description currently wins. Revisability therefore supplies a candidate enabling condition, not a sufficient criterion of realizing emptiness.” This is proposed original argumentation, not an attributed quotation or an empirical result.
