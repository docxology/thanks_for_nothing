# Scene gaze and inquiry into a boundary

This is a new finite experiment, distinct from source-notebook reconstruction
and human eye tracking. [Its GNN declaration](../src/thanks_for_nothing/models/boundary_gaze.gnn.md)
imports admitted gaze channels, geometry, policy coefficients and actual
Realizing Emptiness support. [Generated facts](../output/reports/research_summary.md)
supply current cardinalities, query budgets, cohorts and comparison counts.

## Cognitive situation and learnable quantities

Z is a reference scene, H an encoding, and W the appearance produced by the
declared cyclic map. An independent uniform prior and the admitted maps make
scene queries alone informative about appearance while leaving encoding odds
unchanged. This conclusion is conditional on those assumptions; it is not a
general assertion about vision or self/world boundaries.

A structural question returns a randomized action and its conditional response.
Each marginal is uniform, while the paired relation can identify H.
Different declared reliabilities and costs create an explicit information/cost
choice. The nonspatial question is not a saccade or a recorded human metacognitive act.

The label-only regime removes H from every observation law. Posterior encoding
odds then retain their prior ratio. Larger independent cohorts cannot distinguish
those labels. That impossibility differs from the informative diagnostic regime.

## Paired policies and a shared budget

Visual-only sampling uses the imported gaze policy over appearance.
Integrated sampling considers spatial locations and encoding questions jointly.
Boundary-only sampling can learn an encoding without learning the reference scene.
Uniform sampling is an open same-budget comparator. Restricted sampling retains
actual zero support, so an excluded encoding remains unavailable.

Every policy receives matched complete potential outcomes within a replicate.
The sham follows the integrated query schedule, matching observations and
expenditure, while its updater discards the randomized-action component.
It does not evaluate the demonstrator schedule's likelihood. Its conditional
identity is not a claim that the whole adaptive schedule is information-free.

The overview and main situation figure separate these evidence routes from the
assessment targets. Detailed matrices show why a relation can be informative
although each marginal is not. Spatial paths and nonspatial decisions use
different visual representations.

## Separate assessment targets and units

Encoding Brier loss assesses the final H posterior. Anchored excess log loss
tests reference prediction under the explicitly declared reference intervention.
Current-scene loss, query allocation, entropy and expenditure remain separate.
A better encoding score alone does not establish usable transfer.

Independent replicates remain paired within fixed regime/encoding/scene cells.
Fixed scene strata are equally weighted. Whole paired vectors enter each
exploratory region; policy conditions and repeated decisions do not enlarge N.
The complete family retains both endpoints, direct comparator contrasts,
mechanism-exact offsets and unavailable sample constants.

Nested sample prefixes reuse earlier units and are therefore correlated.
Their Monte Carlo standard errors describe independent-unit precision.
An increase in N cannot repair excluded support or observation equivalence.

## Native checks and interpretation

[The upstream dossier](boundary-gaze-research.md) records actual QRF, channel-ledger
and Gaussian boundary APIs. They are separate mathematical fixtures, not extra
observations in this experiment. Marginal-threshold failure does not imply
failure to recover precision from the known full covariance.

Gates replay both joint episodes/statistics and upstream receipts. Tests alter
likelihoods and pair identities, challenge sham/exact claims, retain label-only
non-identification and reproduce numerical cancellation controls.
[Joint uncertainty](joint-uncertainty.md) defines the interval engine.

The term metacognitive gaze is an operational metaphor for asking about a supplied
encoding. This model does not learn a metacognitive architecture or representation
language, and does not measure sensitivity, efficiency or contemplative realization.
The [empirical-design supplement](manuscript/supplement.md#sec-empirical) specifies
the separate human measurements and identification requirements.
