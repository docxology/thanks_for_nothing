# Evidence and claim boundaries

The machine-readable [claim contract](../data/claim_contract.json) maps each
research claim to an artifact, qualification gate, prohibited inference, future
evidence requirement and a boundary stressor. The contract makes the scope
reviewable; it does not replace scientific judgment or exhaustive language review.

The preserved source paper is a preprint version. Its journal successor has a
verified publication record, but journal full-text equivalence has not been
established. Source reconstruction executes selected numeric notebook cells
through an explicit seeded headless adapter. The source remains unchanged.
See the [notebook audit](notebook-audit.md) for executed findings and static
observations, including implementation choices that are valid.

The corrected experiment has independent simulated agents and paired conditions.
It controls world exposure, baseline learning, replay dose and assessment data.
Its outcomes are beliefs, declared optimism readouts, proper scores and reference
values. None is a validated measure of human gratitude, compassion, well-being,
clinical benefit, neural criticality or contemplative attainment.

The philosophical extension makes four operations explicit. An observed absence
has an observation likelihood. A missing observation preserves the prior under
this model's convention. Appreciation relative to a hypothetical harm is a value
calculation. Consistent state-label permutation checks representational
invariance. Their distinction is useful precisely because no mathematical or
philosophical equivalence among them is assumed.

The central conceptual proposal additionally values and practices examination
of structural commitments as an orientation toward realizing emptiness. Its
finite counterpart separates preference, costly activity, effective access,
evidence-based retention/revision and independent prediction. The authored
reversal gives an omitted candidate a resolving role; its advantage cannot be
transported to all contexts or called a realization score. Upstream practice
deltas remain metadata; the upstream revision diagnostic scores its fitting
windows. New held-out results and cost assumptions belong to this project.
Useful-prior retention and blocked-access nulls constrain claims of progress.

Exhaustive count enumeration is a separate conditional result given effective
inquiry. Rational evidence decisions and binomial masses have no sampling error;
logarithms and losses retain numerical evaluation error. It excludes the repeated
policy's history, availability and costs. A rare stationary open selection has
positive probability and can worsen prediction. Finite menu selection does not
establish absolute model adequacy, parameter recovery or philosophical
sufficiency: even a flexible agent could reify its newest representation.

The attention model changes likelihood precision exogenously. It invokes the real
upstream executor and active-inference agent, while separately distinguishing
matched agent/process qualification from a comparison with a fixed environmental
process. That comparator contains no learned transfer. The separate
[gaze extension](gaze-experiments.md) now embeds frozen learned channels and
retains an assessment-readout alternative. Its direction depends on the bridge,
and greater positive-looking probability can worsen exact whole-scene risk.
Active gaze experiments condition on access to structural alternatives; their
reversal advantage does not identify a distinct inquiry-specific benefit over
open utility or uniform open sampling. These are exploratory finite comparisons,
without human gaze, pixel-based inference or realization measurements.

Bootstrap intervals describe independent simulation units conditional on the fixed
world grid. Sign-flip tests assume symmetric or exchangeable paired differences
under their null. Monte Carlo intervals describe numerical tail-probability
uncertainty. Six mathematical calibration fixtures characterize tested conditions,
including skew/discreteness that violate sign symmetry. Unavailable studentized
intervals count as noncoverage and retain their failure states. Exploratory
world-specific intervals are pointwise; weighting ranges hold estimated means
fixed and are not confidence intervals. None provides empirical power for gratitude.
