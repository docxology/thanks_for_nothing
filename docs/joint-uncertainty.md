# Joint uncertainty and its limits

One exploratory gaze region combines learned-transfer and active-scene contrasts
without replacing their pointwise estimates or the primary recall family.
The separate [scene/encoding study](boundary-gaze-design.md) uses the same
mathematical engine with its own family and blocks. Current family sizes,
confidence levels and draw budgets are [generated](../output/reports/research_summary.md)
from their admitted records.

These analyses were designed after earlier results were inspected.
Multiplicity adjustment does not make them preregistered or establish model adequacy.

## Independent units and retained dependence

Transfer resamples whole paired agents within fixed world strata, preserving
both bridges, policy comparisons and endpoints at the same selected indices.
Active contexts use independent streams; each replicate is first averaged across
its repeated windows. Equal replicate integers across contexts do not imply
cross-context pairing.

All independent blocks contribute to the same maximum in a joint draw.
Locations, observations, windows and contrast columns are not extra independent
subjects. [Paired-vector admission](../src/thanks_for_nothing/gaze_samples.py)
rejects missing/duplicate identities and mismatched potential outcomes.

## Standardized empirical-bootstrap rectangle

The [joint engine](../src/thanks_for_nothing/simultaneous.py) resamples complete
vectors within strata, centers resampled means at fitted means and divides by
each contrast's original standard error. The maximum absolute standardized root
over positive-variance columns determines the common critical value. Its empirical
quantile rank derives from the admitted confidence and draw count.

This is a single-step standardized empirical-bootstrap rectangle. It is distinct
from the resample-studentized bootstrap t interval and from a Romano-Wolf stepdown
test. It does not add covariance jitter or require nonsingular contrast covariance.

Fixed stratum origins stabilize the variance and centered bootstrap calculation.
A near-constant known-answer fixture reproduces the previous cancellation error
and checks the result against an exactly centered representation.

## Monte Carlo precision and exact identities

A conservative binomial order-statistic bracket describes the conditional
critical quantile's Monte Carlo precision. Ties are retained. An unavailable rank
bound remains unbounded rather than being silently replaced. With no stochastic
column there is no critical value and no artificial bootstrap draw.

Exact valuation and sham identities are checked against complete paired source
records before licensing constants. Other observed sample constants remain
unavailable, even if their sample difference is zero. The region reports
complete, partial or unavailable status with every member retained.

A rare-event sample can be constant while population variance is positive.
It must not receive a zero-width stochastic interval. Mechanism identities and
sample coincidences have different evidential status.

## Known-answer operating checks

The fixtures span independent/correlated Gaussian, heterogeneous, skewed and
rare-event families with known contrast means. Their distributional constants
are independent mathematical controls; GNN owns the numerical experiment budgets.
The [typed record](../output/data/gaze_uncertainty.json) retains every setting,
rate denominator, availability state and interval.

Coverage requires every target in an independent experiment to be covered.
Unavailable families count as noncoverage; availability is also displayed.
The unadjusted analytic comparator uses that same all-target event. Exact binomial
bounds and Monte Carlo standard errors qualify rates, not human treatment effects.

Tests retain exact small bootstrap laws, duplicated/opposite columns, disrupted
pairing, independent blocks, discrete quantile brackets and false exact-zero
licenses. Repaired checksum inventories cannot rescue forged scientific records.
Undercoverage and unavailable rare-event results remain findings.

## Reading the figures

Pointwise and joint intervals use different markers. Exact values and unavailable
estimates retain separate symbols. Coverage and availability have separate panels.
The global family is not reduced to the subset selected for a main figure.

The [statistics guide](statistics.md) defines pointwise procedures. The
[source reading dossier](scholarship-v060-research.md) records the methodological
lineage and its access limits. Neither larger simulated N nor a joint region
establishes gratitude efficacy, realization or universal finite-sample coverage.
