# Relational gratitude and first-person measurement

Verified 2026-10-20 for the final targeted scholarship pass. No new quotations
are proposed. These sources fill specific evidential roles, rather than
validating the implemented model or a contemplative attainment.

## Relational gratitude

Algoe, Sara B., Jonathan Haidt and Shelly L. Gable (2008), *Beyond Reciprocity:
Gratitude and Relationships in Everyday Life*, **Emotion** 8(3), 425–429.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/1528-3542.8.3.425);
[primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18540759/).

The abstract describes naturalistic sorority gift-giving and subsequent
relationship ratings. Use it for that narrow association, not causal mediation
or population-wide effects. Access: abstract; live PMC opens returned a browser
challenge, the indexed NYU PDF returned 404, and the old UCSB publication page
redirected to its replacement site. Search-index full-text snippets were not
treated as a completed full-text inspection.

Suggested key: `algoe2008`. Insert in the social-gratitude supplement and briefly
connect it to the main text's distinction between a declared value and a human
construct. Retain the existing `algoe2012` theory citation separately.

## First-person data in a visual task

Lutz, Antoine, Jean-Philippe Lachaux, Jacques Martinerie and Francisco J. Varela
(2002), *Guiding the study of brain dynamics by using first-person data: Synchrony
patterns correlate with ongoing conscious states during a simple visual task*,
**PNAS** 99(3), 1586–1591.
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.032658199);
[NLM identity and abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11805299/);
[inspected primary article, mirrored early-edition PDF](https://journalpsyche.org/articles/0xc0b0.pdf).

Inspected Framework and Methods in the six-page primary article. Trained
participants' reports defined experiential trial groups for behavioral/EEG
analysis. This is a method example, not a validation of emptiness or gratitude.
Publisher/PMC live access was unavailable; the mirrored article's title, authors
and DOI match NLM. No redistribution permission is inferred from accessibility.

Suggested key: `lutz2002`. Use in the main empirical-translation discussion and
the human-task supplement. Keep reports, behavioral prediction and philosophical
interpretation distinct; the model has no EEG measurement or lived experience.
