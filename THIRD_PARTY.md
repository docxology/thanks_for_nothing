## Scene artwork

`data/artwork/scream-original.jpg` preserves the acquired reproduction of Edvard
Munch's *The Scream* (1893), accession NG.M.00939. Photograph:
Nasjonalmuseet / Børre Høstland, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The underlying artwork is identified as public domain by Commons. The
[museum record](https://www.nasjonalmuseet.no/en/collection/object/NG.M.00939),
[Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Edvard_Munch_-_The_Scream_-_NG.M.00939_-_National_Museum_of_Art,_Architecture_and_Design.jpg),
original download URL, dimensions, length and checksum are recorded in
`data/artwork/provenance.json`. Original bytes are unmodified. Scientific display
resamples the complete frame and adds model trajectories, titles and annotations;
these changes are credited in each painting figure. The asset and its provenance
are included in the wheel. Other third-party receipts retain their
original rights and are not covered by the project's MIT license.
