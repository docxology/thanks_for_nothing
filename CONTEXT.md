# Research contract

The primary starting points are Fisher, Sandved Smith and Hohwy's
*Computational mechanisms of gratitude practice*, PsyArXiv version xm25y_v1,
and the supplied belief-updating Colab notebook with 1000 synthetic agents.
The notebook is acquired locally as data/sources/gratitude_original.ipynb (SHA256
1b8a0400ba6c457d1da8ca28a369f60be2f367fb760a794c78ce7125a321ceb6).
It is omitted from the public snapshot; docs/external-inputs.md documents exact
acquisition and the source-license boundary.

Research questions:

1. Which results follow from the source's stated inference and learning rules,
   and which depend on its notebook implementation or experimental design?
2. What changes when sampling, priors, learning and evaluation are explicit,
   reproducible, paired and statistically calibrated?
3. Can a finite model distinguish an observed absence, a missing observation,
   an outcome evaluated relative to a counterfactual, and non-reification of a
   conceptual model? These are distinct operational definitions of nothingness.
4. Can gratitude-inspired precision allocation and appreciation of an avoided
   harm coexist with calibrated uncertainty, without equating optimism or a
   numerical surrogate with human gratitude or contemplative realization?
5. Can appreciation for the conditions of inquiry orient practice toward realizing
   emptiness? The new finite counterpart separates valuing examinability, costly
   action, access to alternatives, conditional retention/revision and independent
   prediction. Aspiration, activity and a numerical score are distinct.
6. Can scene sampling and questions about its predictive encoding jointly support
   reference-frame transfer? The separate boundary-gaze experiment distinguishes
   informative action-response contingencies, observation-equivalent labels,
   restricted support and a matched-query sham. Metacognitive gaze is a finite
   structural-inquiry operation, not a measured human meta-awareness construct.

GNN provides declared model inputs and upstream parsing/validation. Realizing
Emptiness provides tested source admission and finite mathematical comparators.
The new package provides task-specific mechanisms and controls, including the
GNN-declared enacted-practice experiment. Its structural-prior comparison refits
both posteriors; it does not invoke an analytic posterior-reuse BMR identity.
Upstream practice deltas are metadata, and upstream revision scores are in-sample;
the new cost, policy and held-out assessment are explicit project additions. Results are
synthetic model experiments; manuscript language must keep those boundaries.
